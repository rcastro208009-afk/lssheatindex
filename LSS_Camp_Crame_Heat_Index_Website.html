Heat index classification & action

Below 27°C · No heat alertMaintain normal precautions and hydration.
27–32°C · CautionFatigue may occur with prolonged exposure. Hydrate and take breaks.
33–41°C · Extreme CautionHeat cramps and heat exhaustion are possible. Limit outdoor activity.
42–51°C · DangerAvoid strenuous outdoor work; seek shade and rest.
52°C and above · Extreme DangerHeat stroke is imminent. Stop outdoor activity and seek a cool area.

Category ranges follow DOST-PAGASA heat-index guidance.

Safety reminders

  • Drink water regularly.
  • Schedule strenuous work during cooler hours.
  • Rest in shaded, cool, or ventilated areas.
  • Wear breathable clothing and sun protection.
  • Watch for dizziness, headache, cramps, nausea, or weakness.
  • Seek emergency medical assistance for suspected heat stroke.

Data source & update
